Design a modern, premium and highly visual mobile travel planning application called "Trypo".

TRYPO is an all-in-one travel planning app that helps travelers organize their entire journey in one place: destinations, day-by-day itineraries, hostels and hotels, transportation, activities, restaurants, bookings and travel budgets.

The application should feel like a combination of Polarsteps, Stippl and Wanderlog, but with a unique and original identity.

- Minimal clicks
- Easy trip organization
- Beautiful travel imagery
- Intuitive itinerary management
- Smooth navigation
- Social and collaborative travel planning

The itinerary and trip overview should be the strongest and most visually impressive parts of the application.

Make Trypo feel like a real premium startup ready to launch on the App Store.

Create the design as a complete and consistent mobile app design system with reusable components, buttons, cards, navigation elements, input fields and modals.
